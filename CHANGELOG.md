# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- Forked from Forever Quest Tint 0.5.0 by Alex Diakovsky (xanastar) and renamed to **Forever Changes** (`/fchanges`). Settings do not carry over, and the old `ForeverQuestTint` folder can be deleted.
- Items: items that are new to Forever get the teal glow at the bottom of their tooltip. It can be turned off in the options.
- Spells: spells that are new in Forever or changed from Classic get the teal glow in the spellbook, at the trainer and in their tooltips.
- The tooltip of a changed spell lists in orange how it was in vanilla (cost, cast time, cooldown, range, description), where that is known exactly. Both can be turned off in the options.
- Removed the WoW Forever logo option and its artwork.
- The quest name marker icon is drawn without a font.

## Forever Quest Tint (up to 0.5.0)
- Teal gradient over the quest text background (quest window and Map & Quest Log) for quests that were not in original Classic, with a settings panel for colour, opacity and fade length. Works with every Quest Text Contrast setting; on Black the tint is a flat, deeper teal glow.
- Quest name marker: an infinity sign after the name of those quests in the quest log list and the objective tracker, with options for icon or text symbol, size, position, spacing and colour.
- Optional recolouring of the objective lines of those quests.
